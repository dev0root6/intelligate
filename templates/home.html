How It Works

Three simple steps from booking to boarding

1. Book Your Ticket

Select your route, add passengers, and capture each face using your webcam.

2. Walk to the Gate

The gate camera scans your face and matches it against your booking instantly.

3. Walk Through

If your ticket is valid and unused, the gate opens automatically. That's it.

Why IntelliGate?

Replacing QR codes with facial recognition makes metro travel faster, more secure, and completely hands-free.

  • No physical tickets or QR scans needed
  • One face capture links to your booking
  • Automatic gate control via ESP32
  • Ticket status tracking prevents reuse
